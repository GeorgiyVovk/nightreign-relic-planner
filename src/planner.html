What's active

Multipliers multiply, as in FromSoft games. Damage per hit: attack × buffs → enemy negation → frostbite. Attack is what the game's menu shows: 60% of the stat formula, and every enemy has defense 100, which lets exactly that through. Weapon attack, spell scaling and plain hits match the game on a Training Post without relics; buffs come from the data and aren't all checked in game. A weapon attack is the whole chain you pick in the Damage panel (R1, R2 or charged R2), hit by hit, at the motion value and physical type the game gives that moveset; a spell is its first hit. Weapons, spells and enemies come from the game's 1.03.5 data. Other numbers: Nightreign Useful Info v1.03.2 and the Hexinton cheat table.
